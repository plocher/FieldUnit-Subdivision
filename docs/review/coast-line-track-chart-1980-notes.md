# Coast Line track chart, 1980: regime limits between Gilroy and Watsonville Junction

Research note, 2026-11-10. Source opened: *Southern Pacific Coast Line, SF to LA, 1980-1982 Track Chart*,
`https://www.spcoast.com/spins/Southern%20Pacific%20Coast%20Line%20-%20SF%20to%20LA%201980-1982%20Track%20Chart.pdf`,
sheets 307 to 310 (PDF pages 9 to 12), each "REVISED TO 1-1-80". Read from the scan at 220 dpi; the PDF's text
layer is unusable. Second source opened: GCOR 8th edition, 1 April 2020 (Army-hosted copy; UP's copy is
`https://www.up.com/ert/gcor.pdf`). SPCoast's era is the 1985 GCOR, whose numbering may differ `(unverified)`.

Reading: in 1980 CTC governed the single track from Corporal to Logan, with Sargent a siding inside it, and the
single track south of Watsonville Junction. The double track Gilroy to Corporal and Logan to Watsonville Junction lay
outside the CTC brackets. The chart does not state the double-track rule; the timetable designates such limits
(GCOR 10.1: "CTC limits are designated in the timetable"). Current of traffic with automatic block is the usual
arrangement for the era `(unverified)` until a period timetable is opened.

Station marks: a signal/interlocking mark "S" is drawn at Gilroy (80.40), Corporal (86.35) and Watsonville Junction
(100.58), at the double-track ends.

## What GCOR 8th edition says (opened, quoted)

- 10.1 Authority to Enter CTC Limits: "CTC limits are designated in the timetable. Sidings within CTC limits are
  controlled sidings and are governed by CTC rules."
- 14.0 Rules Applicable Only Within Track Warrant Control (TWC) Limits; 14.2 Designated Limits.
- 16.1 Authority to Enter DTC Limits: "The timetable will designate DTC limits." DTC "will not include territory where
  Rule 6.13 (Yard Limits) or Rule 6.14 (Restricted Limits) is in effect."
- 6.13 Yard Limits; 6.14 Restricted Limits; 9.14 Movement with the Current of Traffic; 9.15 Track Permits.
- Glossary, Interlocking Limits: "The tracks between outer opposing absolute signals of an interlocking." This is the
  ADR 0013 §13 seam.

## Consequences for SPCoast

- The boundary sign at Corporal faces south: a northbound leaving the single track reads END CTC, a southbound
  entering it reads BEGIN CTC. The regime north of Corporal is the double-track rule, not TWC.
- Sargent on the prototype is a CTC siding, not APB single track with a controlled switch. The SPCoast drawing may
  choose otherwise; it is the R&D testbed, accepted as not prototypically correct (owner, 2026-11-10).
- Regime markers are `GCOR Rule NN` POLICY symbols on a piece of track, extent by derivation (joint-bounded pieces;
  marker, interlocking membership, or inheritance across a joint; conflicts are errors). The sign faces are rendered
  from the two adjacent regimes. Proposed amendment to ADR 0009 §3.12 and ADR 0013 §7.1 and decision 3, pending.

## The prototypical Coast Line project

Starts at the TestCorpus split (Subdivision #66), San Jose Newhall to Watsonville Junction, trackplan and office
