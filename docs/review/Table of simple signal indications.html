In railroad signaling (AAR and GCOR), the difference between an aspect and an indication is the difference between what a signal looks like and what a train crew must do in response to it.

Core Definitions
  • Aspect: The visual appearance of a fixed signal as viewed from the direction of an approaching train. This includes the combination of color, position, flashing lights, or shape of the signal head.
  • Indication: The operating rule or command dictated by the aspect. It tells the engineer exactly how to control the speed and movement of the train.
