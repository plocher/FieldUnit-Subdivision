Historical plant note

Carnadero, Luchessa, and Gilroy Industrial.

< Railroad West/North                             Railroad East/South >
                                                oo--| 2NEF
                    2SCD |--oo              +====== ================= Hollister Branch
                        = ====\            /
                           3T1 \   [MC]   / 5T1 oo--| 2NCD
                   /======= ====+==== ===+========= ==========2NA===<  MT2
              1T1 /                             oo--| 2AB
    MT   =1SA= ==+================================= ==========1NA===>  MT1
              |--oo 2SAB

Single track continues north of Luchessa. Double track south is operated
using current-of-traffic, Rule 251 territory. Turnout 1 divides single
track into double track. Turnout 3 leads to Gilroy Industrial. Turnout 5
leads to the Hollister Branch, which is dark except for its approach.

Eastbound approach: Gilroy Interchange 1SA.
Westbound approaches: Hollister 1NA and Christopher 2NA.
