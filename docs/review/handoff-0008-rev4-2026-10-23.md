## The work, in order

1. **A reader** over the project's netlist that produces the seam facts of 0009: transports (with their anchoring
   processors, one per placed instance), encodings (on which transport instance; code line or I/O), processors
   (what they run, what they anchor, their bus), units and houses (line, station addresses derived per §3.8),
   nodes (transport, UA, declaration, bits on their bus), drivers on buses, head devices to trackplan heads. It is
   the first piece of the milestone D compiler, and the example in 0008 must be its output, never typed.
2. **The schema's new major**: entities transports, codelines (encoding on transport), ioEncodings, processors,
   nodes, buses; bindings as routes (`bus owner / address / use`); stations derived; `fieldUnit.processor`,
   `officeUnit.processor`; the machine's columns, not lines; keys as in 0009 §3.10; canonical form as in 0008 §4.8.
3. **ADR 0008 rev 4**: §4.5, §4.9, §5, §6 rewritten on 0009; pictures in `docs/adr/0008/` redrawn (the shape on
   the FieldUnit layers picture); `README.md` row; one ask: ratify. Keep rev 3's record; add the rev 4 entry.
4. **An adversarial review** by a fresh agent anchored on 0009 and the glossary, not on the writer's reasoning,
   as `docs/review/naming-audit-2026-10-22.md` was done. Then the owner's ratification.
