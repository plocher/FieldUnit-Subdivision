# Layout Data Model File 3.3.0: a mast's atoms

The migration note of 3.3.0 (ADR 0008 C8; the owner, 2026-11-12, on the first consumer's findings). A minor bump: a
3.2.0 file written by the KiCad importer validates against 3.3.0 but for its `schema` string and the shape of a mast's
`derived.positions`, which is rebuilt by any conforming derivation (ADR 0011 §4: `derived` is a cache). A file is not
migrated: it is written again by the importer from the drawing.

## Why

A mast's Name (`784WBC`) is a composite of three atoms: the signal number, the side it faces and its head letters top
down (ADR 0003 rules 5 to 7). Until 3.2.0 the derivation and every consumer read the Name: a desk lever 784 found its
masts by cutting the letters off, and a head's railroad letter could only be had by counting letters in the Name, since
`positions` was keyed by the pin letter A, B, C, which shares an alphabet with the head letters (784WBC's pin A is head
B). Deriving from the Name denormalizes: every Studio edit would touch many stored things. The normal form: the atoms
are stated, the Name is a label, the KiCad importer decomposes the drawn Name (ADR 0011: shorthand becomes facts in the
importer), and nothing downstream parses a Name.

## What changed

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| appliance mast (new) | `given.signal`: the signal number, `784` |
| appliance mast (new) | `given.heads`: the head letters top down, a comma list, `B,C`; the k-th letter is pin k |
| appliance mast `relations.facing`: the glyph's turn | the Name's side letter (N, W `LEFT`; S, E `RIGHT`); the glyph must agree (import diagnostic `geometry.facing`). A train order board, and a mast whose Name is not the grammar, keep the glyph's turn |
| appliance mast `derived.positions`: pin -> the words it presents | pin -> `{letter, presents}`: the letter for that pin from `given.heads`, the words from the heads wired to it. A lettered pin with no head wired has no `presents`; a wired pin beyond the letters has no `letter` |
| device head (new) | `derived.letter`: its mast's letter for its `relations.position`; derived, never stated on the head |
| device lever, Style signal (new) | `derived.masts`: the masts of its interlocking whose `given.signal` is its number |

Rule checks: `mast.lever` (missing: a CONTROLLED mast whose signal number no desk signal lever serves) and
`reference.switches` (warning: a wire joins switch ports of two interlockings with no joint between) are new;
`mast.heads`, `mast.letters` and `lever.serves` read the stated atoms; `mast.grammar` and `mast.facing` are retired
(the Name's grammar and the glyph's turn are the importer's: `mast.name` at missing, `geometry.facing` at warning).
`tools/layout_model/derive.py` no longer imports a Name-reading function for masts.
